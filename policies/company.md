# Company Policy

## About us
We are an online shop that sells products directly to customers. This policy explains how we run our store and how we treat our customers. Our cancellation, refund and shipping rules are described in their own policies.

## Customer support
- Contact our support team with your order number so we can find your order quickly.
- Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. We do not answer on weekends or public holidays.
- We reply to every request within **2 business days**.

## Customer accounts
- You need an account to place an order. Use your real name, phone number and delivery address so we can deliver your order.
- Keep your password private. We never ask for your password by email, phone or chat.
- You can view and update your own details in your account at any time.

## Payment
- Prices are shown in US dollars.
- The price you see when you place the order is the price you pay, even if we change the price later.
- We do not store your full card details.

## Product information
We try to keep product descriptions, prices and stock levels accurate. If we find a mistake in the price or description of an order that is still **Pending**, we contact you before we confirm it, and you may cancel the order for free.

## Privacy
- We collect only the information needed to process your orders and support requests: your name, email, phone number and delivery address.
- We use your information only to deliver your orders and to help you. We do not sell your personal information.
- You can ask support to correct your details.

## Fair use
We may refuse or cancel orders that look fraudulent or abusive, and we refund any payment already made for them.

## Changes to our policies
We may update our policies from time to time. The policy in force when you place an order applies to that order.
